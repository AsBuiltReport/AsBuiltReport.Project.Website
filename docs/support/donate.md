# Support AsBuiltReport

AsBuiltReport is a free, open-source project built and maintained by the community. Your support helps ensure the project continues to grow and improve.

## Financial Support

If you wish to show your financial support for this project, donations are greatly appreciated and help cover:

- **Infrastructure Costs** - Hosting, domain registration, and testing environments
- **Development Time** - Maintaining and improving the core framework and documentation
- **New Features** - Adding support for new platforms and technologies
- **Community Support** - Responding to issues, questions, and feature requests

## Alternative Ways to Contribute

Can't contribute financially? There are many other valuable ways to support the project:

**1. Contribute Code**

Help improve AsBuiltReport by:

- **Fixing bugs** - Tackle issues in existing report modules
- **Adding features** - Implement new capabilities or enhancements
- **Creating report modules** - Build support for new vendors or technologies
- **Improving documentation** - Enhance guides, examples, and troubleshooting content

See the [Contributing Guide](../dev-guide/contributing.md) to get started.

**2. Report Issues & Suggest Features**

Help make AsBuiltReport better by:

- Reporting bugs you encounter
- Suggesting new features or improvements
- Providing feedback on documentation
- Sharing your use cases and requirements

Visit the [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/AsBuiltReport/discussions) to share your ideas.

**3. Help Others**

Support the AsBuiltReport community by:

- Answering questions in GitHub Discussions
- Sharing your experiences and best practices
- Creating blog posts or tutorials
- Presenting at user groups or conferences

**4. Spread the Word**

Help grow the project by:

- Starring the [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/AsBuiltReport)
- Sharing AsBuiltReport on social media
- Recommending it to colleagues and peers
- Writing reviews or case studies

**5. Test & Provide Feedback**

Help ensure quality by:

- Testing new releases and providing feedback
- Validating report accuracy in your environment
- Suggesting improvements to existing modules
- Participating in beta testing programmes

## Corporate Sponsorship

If your organisation benefits from AsBuiltReport and would like to provide ongoing support, please [contact us](../about/contact.md) to discuss sponsorship opportunities.

Corporate sponsors may receive:

- Recognition on the project website
- Priority support for bug fixes and feature requests
- Custom development consideration
- Joint promotion opportunities

## Transparency

All financial contributions go directly toward:

- Hosting and infrastructure costs
- Development tools and licenses
- Testing environments and resources

## Thank You

Your support - whether financial, code contributions, or community participation - makes AsBuiltReport possible. Thank you for being part of this project!

## Questions?

If you have questions about donations or other ways to contribute, please visit our [Contact page](../about/contact.md) or join the conversation in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/AsBuiltReport/discussions).
